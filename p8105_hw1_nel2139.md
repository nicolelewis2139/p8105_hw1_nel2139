## Description of the dataset

The `penguins` dataset includes measurements for penguin species, island
in Palmer Archipelago, size (flipper length, body mass, bill
dimensions), and sex. The dataset includes the following variables;
`species`, `island`, `bill_len` `bill_dep`, `flipper_len`, `body_mass`,
`sex`, and `year`. The species are Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo, and the
islands are Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen. Bill length ranges from 32.1 to
59.6 mm, bill depth ranges from 13.1 to 21.5 mm, flipper length ranges
from 172 to 231 mm, body mass ranges from 2700 to 6300 g. Sex is
recorded as female, male, with some values missing, and the data were
collected from 2007 to 2009.

The dataset has 344 rows and 8 columns.

The mean flipper length is 200.9 mm.
